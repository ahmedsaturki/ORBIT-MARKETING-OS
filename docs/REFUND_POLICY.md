# ORBIT Marketing OS — Refund Policy Baseline

This is a draft baseline and requires legal and payment-provider review before publication.

## Subscription plans

Cancellations, renewals, and subscription refunds follow the payment provider's rules plus the policy displayed at checkout.

## Lifetime purchase

Lifetime access applies to the product scope stated at purchase. It does not guarantee permanent compatibility with operating systems, third-party platforms, app stores, or external services.

## Exceptional cases

Duplicate charges, unauthorized charges, and verified payment-processing errors should be handled through the payment provider and support channel.

No refund policy is active in production until a final reviewed version with the actual merchant details is published.
